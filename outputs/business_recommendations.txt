Business Recommendations Report

Demand Forecasting
------------------
- Use forecasted demand trends to align procurement and production schedules with expected sales volume.
- Prioritize SKU replenishment for items with sustained upward demand forecasts to prevent stockouts.
- Review forecast accuracy regularly and adjust model inputs where prediction variance exceeds acceptable thresholds.
- Incorporate seasonality, promotion, and regional demand patterns into planning cycles to improve forecast confidence.

Inventory Optimization
----------------------
- Reorder stock for SKUs with positive inventory gaps and "Restock Required" status before lead-time windows close.
- Maintain safety stock for high variability SKUs to reduce risk of service-level failures.
- Identify slow-moving or overstocked SKUs and reduce order quantities or run targeted promotions to free working capital.
- Use reorder point and inventory gap metrics to prioritize purchasing actions and minimize both stockout and excess inventory costs.

Customer Retention Strategy
---------------------------
- Focus retention campaigns on customers classified as "High Risk" with immediate, personalized outreach.
- Offer tailored incentives and loyalty programs for medium-risk customers to increase engagement and repeat orders.
- Maintain regular engagement for low-risk customers to preserve relationships and prevent churn.
- Monitor churn label trends continuously and use customer lifetime value signals to prioritize outreach budgets.

Future Improvements
-------------------
- Integrate demand forecasting outputs directly into inventory optimization workflows for automated replenishment planning.
- Enhance churn modeling with additional behavioral and demographic features to improve risk segmentation accuracy.
- Establish closed-loop feedback between sales forecasts, inventory actions, and retention outcomes to refine business rules.
- Add automated reporting dashboards for real-time visibility into forecast performance, inventory health, and customer churn metrics.
